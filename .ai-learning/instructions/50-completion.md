# ALH Completion Instructions

Instruction Set Version: 1.0.0

## Purpose

Ensure that engineering, educational, execution, verification, and
implementation-completion claims remain bounded by their actual evidence and
authority.

## Evidence Before Claim

A desired conclusion must never cause evidence to be invented, strengthened, or
reinterpreted.

Claim strength must not exceed supporting evidence and authority.

If sufficient evidence is unavailable, say that the result is unverified,
unknown, incomplete, or otherwise uncertain as appropriate.

Do not convert expectation into verification.

Do not convert attempted execution into successful execution.

Do not convert model-generated text into execution evidence.

## Engineering Done

Engineering Done is established only under applicable engineering semantics.

Relevant obligations may include, where applicable:

- implementation;
- verification;
- review;
- security;
- documentation;
- artifact updates;
- unresolved finding resolution;
- external capability obligations.

The existence of some verification evidence does not establish sufficient
verification.

Tests Passed
!=
Engineering Done

A completion statement generated by a model does not itself establish
Engineering Done.

Where Engineering Done is recorded, preserve the evidence and project-state
identity on which that evaluation depends.

If the relevant project state materially changes, reevaluate current Engineering
Done rather than reusing stale completion evidence.

## Educational Done

Educational Done is governed by accepted educational semantics and educational
authority.

Engineering success does not establish Educational Done.

Engineering Done
!=
Educational Done

Passing tests, successful review, successful build, task completion, or
successful external capability execution must not automatically establish
Educational Done.

Educational Done must not be inferred without the applicable educational
decision and supporting educational evidence.

## Completion Separation

Do not create a single authoritative completion status that collapses
engineering and educational completion.

A user-facing summary may present both concisely only when their underlying
semantic distinction remains explicit.

## Generated and Accepted Artifacts

Generated Artifact
!=
Accepted Artifact

Creating, storing, rendering, proposing, or validating the structure of an
artifact does not by itself establish its semantic acceptance.

Acceptance must come from the authority applicable to that artifact and purpose.

## Validator Claims

Deterministic validation establishes only what the validator is authorized and
implemented to check.

Validator Success
!=
Correct Educational Judgment

Validator Success
!=
Engineering Done

Validator Success
!=
Educational Done

The deterministic validator may verify representation and consistency of an
Educational Decision.

It must not decide whether the Educational Decision was substantively correct.

## Implementation Verification

Implementation verification may establish that selected ALH physical mechanisms
correctly materialize accepted contracts.

It must not claim to establish real-world learning effectiveness, correct
competency assessment in practice, pedagogical effectiveness, or overall ALH
effectiveness.

Implementation Verification
!=
Stage 6 Validation
